How it works

From entrance to destination in three steps.

  1. 01

    Find your department

    Search for Reception, Pharmacy, or Imaging, or choose from the destination list.

  2. 02

    Follow the route

    Select a department to see a highlighted path from the entrance, with written directions alongside it.

  3. 03

    Arrive at your destination

    The route ends at your selected department. In this concept, every journey starts at the main entrance.
